**Delivery:** Kore, clear and lightly playful.

## Line 3 — 03-direction (Frame 3)

**Delivery:** Kore, clear and lightly playful.

    This run surfaced fifteen sources. You can open the evidence behind each finding. Gemini connects those signals into a creative opening: what if the building is an unreliable witness? Each room can remember only its own point of view. Now we have a specific storytelling rule to explore, instead of another vague suggestion.

## Line 4 — 04-first-cut (Frame 4)

**Delivery:** Kore, clear and lightly playful.

    Now, agentic video understanding. We attach a real clip, an early location test. Gemini watches the frames and checks them against our idea. At six seconds, it sees an empty corridor. But our story needs a detective and conflicting memories. Yuvo turns that gap into a next move: introduce the detective.

## Line 5 — 05-next-cut (Frame 5)

**Delivery:** Kore, clear and lightly playful.

    We attach a second cut. Yuvo sends both videos to Gemini so it can compare what actually changed. The later start changes the framing. The story still needs a point of view. That is the loop: make a choice, show the cut, and get a concrete next editing move.

## Line 6 — 06-built (Frame 6)

**Delivery:** Charon, conversational and clear.

    Start with your idea. Find a direction. Upload your cut. Yuvo shows what comes across, what is missing, and what to try next. Gemini reviews the video. Parallel grounds the research. You make the final call.
