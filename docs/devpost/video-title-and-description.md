# Title

Yuvo | From a story idea to a better cut

# Description

A woman can talk to abandoned buildings. Their memories are incomplete. How do you turn that premise into something an audience can see?

Meet Yuvo, a creative companion that follows your film from the first idea to the next cut.

Parallel researches related stories and audience reactions. Gemini turns that evidence into a creative direction. Agentic video understanding reviews your footage against the premise. Upload another cut to see what changed and what still needs work.

This demo follows one idea through live research and two video reviews. Waiting times are condensed for clarity.

Built with Gemini on Vertex AI, Google ADK, Parallel and Google Cloud Run for Agentic Cinema: The Blockbuster Hackathon.
