## Video direction

Yuvo paper, ink, yellow, blue, coral and mint; Manrope. Main current LEFT. The seven frames are joined by stamped cut-the-curve left handoffs, never alternating directions. Real captured UI is the proof. The graphics explain the story rather than imitate the app. No grain, texture, fake cursor, float loops, invented citations or fake model responses. Wait time is condensed and disclosed. Every shot progresses through real UI action or staged narration-led reveals. Room-circle diagram in frame 3 is an editorial explanation of the recorded output. Frames 1 and 7 use a brief stillness before the payoff. No burned-in captions over the app; provide a separate English subtitle file.
